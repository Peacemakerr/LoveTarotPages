Privacy Policy

Love Tarot Soulmate · Effective May 8, 2026

Love Tarot Soulmate is an entertainment app for reflective love tarot readings. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Data Collection

We do not collect personal data from this app. The app does not require an account, login, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

Reading Requests

When you request a reading, the app sends the selected spread, card names, card positions, language, and non-identifying reading context to our backend service so that a reading can be generated in real time. We use this request only to provide the reading response.

Local History

Saved readings and daily free-use counters are stored locally on your device. They are not synced to our server. You can delete saved readings inside the app.

Third-Party Services

The app uses a backend service and AI model provider to generate reading text. We do not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking technologies in the app.

Entertainment Notice

Love Tarot Soulmate is for entertainment and self-reflection only. It does not provide professional advice, medical advice, psychological counseling, fortune-telling, or guaranteed relationship outcomes.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
